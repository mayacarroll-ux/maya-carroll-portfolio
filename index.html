Product thinking shaped by years in technology

My background helps me understand both the customer experience and the realities of building software. I bring a practical, collaborative approach to product design—from defining the problem through refining the final experience.

Clarity from complexity

I organize complicated requirements, workflows, and information into experiences people can understand and use.

Cross-functional collaboration

I work closely with product managers, engineers, stakeholders, and customers to build shared understanding and make informed decisions.

End-to-end design

I can contribute across discovery, user flows, information architecture, wireframes, prototypes, interface design, and implementation support.

Technology perspective

More than 20 years in technology gives me valuable context for navigating constraints, communicating with technical teams, and designing solutions that can be built.

Thoughtful design, without unnecessary ceremony

Every project is different, but my process usually begins with understanding the people, business goals, technical constraints, and unanswered questions involved. I use research, mapping, prototyping, and testing where they provide meaningful evidence—not simply to check boxes. The goal is to help the team make better decisions and create a product that works well for the people using it.

01 · UNDERSTAND

Clarify the problem, audience, goals, constraints, and measures of success.

02 · EXPLORE

Map workflows, identify opportunities, and compare possible directions.

03 · MAKE

Turn promising ideas into flows, prototypes, and refined interface designs.

04 · LEARN

Gather feedback, test assumptions, and improve the experience before and after launch.
